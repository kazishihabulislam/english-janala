Frequently Asked Question

What is English Janala?
English Janala is an interactive English vocabulary learning platform that helps learners improve their English words, meanings, pronunciation, and vocabulary skills.
How can I learn vocabulary using English Janala?
Choose a vocabulary level and explore the available words. Each word provides useful information such as its meaning, pronunciation, and other learning details.
Are the vocabulary words divided into different levels?
Yes. English Janala organizes vocabulary into different levels so learners can study words according to their learning stage.
Can I listen to the pronunciation of a word?
Yes. Click the pronunciation button 🔊 to hear the selected English word using your browser's built-in speech synthesis feature.
Does English Janala provide word meanings?
Yes. Each vocabulary item can display the meaning of the English word along with other available information from the vocabulary API.
Where does the vocabulary data come from?
English Janala retrieves vocabulary information dynamically from an API and displays the data on the website.
What happens if a word's meaning is unavailable?
If the meaning is not available, English Janala displays a user-friendly message instead of showing empty or undefined content.
What happens if the requested word cannot be found?
English Janala informs the user that the requested word could not be found and encourages them to try another word.
Do I need to reload the page to see new vocabulary?
No. Vocabulary information is loaded dynamically when you interact with the available vocabulary options.
Can I use English Janala on mobile devices?
Yes. English Janala is designed with a responsive interface so learners can use it on smartphones, tablets, and desktop devices.
